NOVARA V2.18.3 — AUDIT / CHANGE NOTES

Base: V2.18.2 source retrieved from the user's Library.

1) SAME-COIN DUPLICATE / DIRECTION FLIP PROTECTION
- Only one signal per coin is selected in a scan cycle, even if LONG and SHORT pattern candidates both exist.
- A coin with any OPEN paper thesis cannot emit another signal, whether same-side or opposite-side.
- After a closed SL, opposite/same-side re-entry is locked for 90 minutes.
- After TP: 45-minute reset. After AMBIGUOUS/expiry: 60-minute reset.
- Existing 30-minute cross-pattern and 90-minute same-pattern cooldowns remain as additional protection.
- This directly addresses the PONS LONG then SHORT sequence: the second direction is blocked while the first thesis is open and for 90 minutes after its stop-out.

2) TP REACHABILITY — NO MORE REWARD DISTANCE JUST TO PASS R:R
- Structural TP candidates must be within min(7% of entry, max(1.5% of entry, 8 x 1H ATR)).
- Targets more than 3% from entry require at least 3 touches on the 1H level; closer targets still require at least 2 touches.
- TP remains placed before the structural zone using the existing buffer.
- The engine does not pull a target closer artificially or extend it to manufacture a high R:R. If no reachable structural target meets the minimum net R:R, the candidate is rejected.
- The PONS LONG example had a 16.48% target and a 1H resistance with only 2 touches; this is outside the new TP envelope and cannot pass as-is.

3) BTC SPLIT-REGIME / ALTCOIN INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION
- When the BTC scenario explicitly reports a 4H countertrend WAIT state, an altcoin must score at least 6.90 and show at least 2 of: aligned 1H asset trend, directional trade-flow/order-book confirmation, volume >=0.90x.
- When BTC 1H and 15m both oppose the candidate, the alt requires aligned asset trend, volume >=1.15x, directional trade delta, OI change >=+0.15%, and score >=7.10.
- This addresses the two PONS examples: the LONG had neutral asset trend and 0.76x volume; the SHORT had BTC 1H/15m opposing, 1.11x volume, negative OI change, and score 6.84. Both fail the new independent-confirmation requirements.
- These are conservative heuristic guards, not calibrated probabilities.

4) PAPER TRADE TIME EXPIRY
- Default maximum paper thesis duration is 24 hours (configurable 6–72h).
- If neither level is touched by expiry, the trade is closed as AMBIGUOUS with no fabricated TP/SL or R result.
- This prevents old unresolved paper trades from locking a coin indefinitely.
