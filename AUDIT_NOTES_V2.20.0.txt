MODEL DESIGN
The next-move layer produces long/short directional conviction from a bounded
weighted combination of multi-horizon returns, acceleration, EMA spread, trade
delta, L2 imbalance, OI change, VWAP displacement, candle pressure and funding.
A whipsaw penalty reduces conviction for wide-range candles with weak close-to-close
follow-through. It is a deterministic heuristic, not a fitted or calibrated ML model.
